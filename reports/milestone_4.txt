2. HOW WE TUNED WITHOUT CHEATING (STRICT VAULT RULE)
----------------------------------------------------
A common mistake in machine learning is tuning settings directly on the
test set, which artificially inflates scores. To avoid that:
  - Our final 187 test games (2023-01-28 to 2023-02-28) were locked in
    a vault and NEVER touched during tuning.
  - tune_models.py tested 46 configurations strictly inside the first
    744 training games using 5 expanding-window chronological folds
    (124 games per block), re-standardizing features inside every fold.
  - For each model, we picked the setting with the highest average
    training-CV accuracy (breaking ties by ROC-AUC, then Log-Loss).

Search Grids & Winning Settings (from best_hyperparams.json):
  - KNN (8 values of k: 5 to 55):
      Winner -> k = 35  (Train 5-Fold CV Acc: 61.45%)
  - Gaussian NB (8 values of var_smoothing: 1e-9 to 2.0):
      Winner -> var_smoothing = 1.0  (Train 5-Fold CV Acc: 62.10%)
  - Logistic Regression (30 combos of lr, epochs, l2):
      Winner -> lr = 0.05, epochs = 1000, l2 = 0.1  (Train CV: 62.26%)

4. WHAT IMPROVED & WHAT WE LEARNED
----------------------------------
  1) Tuned Gaussian Naive Bayes Was Our Biggest Breakthrough:
     In Milestone 3, we noticed Gaussian NB ranked games well (0.678
     AUC) but had terrible Log-Loss (0.7049) because correlated stats
     made it overconfident. Increasing var_smoothing to 1.0 widened its
     bell curves and fixed that problem completely:
       * Test Accuracy : 62.57% -> 63.64% (+5.35% over baseline, #1!)
       * ROC-AUC       : 0.678  -> 0.688  (#1 overall!)
       * Log-Loss      : 0.7049 -> 0.6188 (-0.0861 drop, #1 overall!)
       * 5-Fold CV Acc : 58.76% -> 62.76% (+4.00 pp jump, #1 overall!)

  2) Light L2 Regularization Sharpened Logistic Regression:
     Adding a small L2 penalty (l2 = 0.1) kept weights stable across
     overlapping stats like win_pct and avg_point_diff:
       * ROC-AUC jumped from 0.663 -> 0.686 (+0.023)
       * F1-Score rose from 74.16% -> 74.73% (#1 overall!)
       * Full-season 5-Fold CV improved from 61.98% -> 62.63%
       * Held steady at 63.10% test accuracy with lower Log-Loss (0.6413).

  3) KNN Showed a Classic Bias-Variance Trade-Off:
     Moving from k=15 to k=35 smoothed out noise and lifted full-season
     5-Fold CV from 59.79% -> 62.37% (+2.58 pp), though on the final
     187-game holdout window it dipped slightly (63.10% -> 62.03%) as
     larger neighborhoods pulled close games toward the home majority.

  4) What Stats Actually Win NBA Games (Feature Importance):
     Looking at Tuned Logistic Regression's largest learned weights:
       * home_avg_point_diff  (+0.102) : Strong home scoring margin
       * visitor_is_b2b       (+0.097) : Road team tired on back-to-back
       * diff_avg_point_diff  (+0.088) : Scoring margin gap between teams
       * home_avg_pts_allowed (-0.064) : Weak home defense helps visitors
       * home_win_pct         (+0.056) : Overall home team win rate
       * home_avg_pts_scored  (+0.056) : Home offensive firepower
       * home_is_b2b          (-0.032) : Home team tired on back-to-back
     These match real basketball intuition: average point differential
     and back-to-back fatigue are stronger predictors than win-loss
     record alone!


5. HONEST LIMITATIONS & CONCLUSION
----------------------------------
Why do even well-tuned NBA models sit around ~63-64% accuracy instead
of 90%? Basketball has huge night-to-night randomness: star players
sitting out for injury/rest, mid-season roster trades, foul trouble,
and hot or cold 3-point shooting nights are not in team-level pre-game
box scores. Even professional Las Vegas oddsmakers typically hit around
66-69% across a full NBA season.

Beating the 58.29% home-court baseline by over +5.3 percentage points
(63.64% accuracy, 0.688 ROC-AUC, 0.6188 Log-Loss) using clean,
from-scratch Python & NumPy models shows that simple, understandable
algorithms can capture genuine basketball signal without heavy black-box
libraries.
